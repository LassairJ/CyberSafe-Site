# About

The **CyberSafe Brief** is a newsletter about staying safe online, written for
people who don't work in tech: parents, grandparents, friends, and anyone who
just wants clear answers without the jargon.

Every issue covers what's actually worth your attention — scams going around,
settings worth changing, and simple habits that prevent most problems.

This site is the permanent home for the Brief's evergreen guides and a
mirrored archive of past issues.
