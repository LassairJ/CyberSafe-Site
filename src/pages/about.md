# About the CyberSafe Brief

Security advice often feels like it was written for IT departments, not everyday people. Between confusing tech jargon and constant headlines telling you who or what got hacked, it’s easy to feel overwhelmed, or to just tune it out completely.

The CyberSafe Brief was built to change that.

The goal is simple: take complex cybersecurity threats, privacy risks, and industry updates, and translate them into practical, plain-English advice that anyone can use to protect themselves and their families. No fear-mongering, no advanced technical degree required.
