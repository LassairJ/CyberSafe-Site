## Conduent Data Breach - 10.4 Million Affected
Business-process giant Conduent said its systems were breached between **October 21, 2024** and **January 13, 2025**, and that **over 10.5 million people** may be impacted. The stolen information includes full names, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, health-insurance IDs, and medical details.

Conduent has begun sending breach notifications starting late October 2025 and filed reports with multiple state attorneys general. The attack is part of a broader trend of major service providers being targeted.

___

### What to Know
- Over **10 million people impacted**; major exposure of sensitive health and identity info.
- The breach happened at a service company that supports many state and enterprise clients - your data could be affected even if you didn’t directly use Conduent.
- Stolen info is **highly valuable** to identity thieves: SSNs + health data = big risk.

### What to Do Now
- Watch for notification letters or emails from Conduent or your state AG’s office.
- Consider putting a credit freeze or a fraud alert on your credit report with the three major bureaus.
- Monitor your statements and health-insurance portal for unexpected activity.
- Be extra cautious with phishing emails or calls: attackers may now have some of your real personal info.
